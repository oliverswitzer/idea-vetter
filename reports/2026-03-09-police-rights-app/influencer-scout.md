# YouTube Influencer Report: Police Rights & Traffic Stop App
**Date:** 2026-03-09
**App Concept:** AI app for recording police interactions, knowing rights during traffic stops, and fighting traffic tickets afterward
**Target Audiences:** Everyday drivers, civil rights-conscious users, people who have had adverse police stop experiences

---

## Methodology

- Generated 5 niche keyword clusters and ran parallel searches across YouTube domain and general web
- Identified 30+ candidate channels across five content niches
- Pulled subscriber counts, view totals, and engagement metrics from vidIQ, Social Blade, SpeakRJ, youtubers.me, HypeAuditor, and Tubics
- Applied engagement filters: engagement rate >= 5%, recent uploads, active within 30 days
- Searched official websites and public records for business contact emails
- Channels are ranked by niche fit to the app concept, then by engagement rate

**Note on engagement rate calculation used here:** Engagement rate = (average views per video / subscriber count) x 100. This is a view-based engagement proxy derived from total views divided by video count, as like/comment counts were not uniformly available across all channels. Where comment-based engagement data was available from analytics platforms, it is noted separately.

## Summary

**Total channels scanned:** 30+ across five niches
**Channels qualifying for this report (engagement >= 5% or strong niche fit):** 12
**Filter notes:** Engagement thresholds were applied in combination with niche relevance. Several channels (PoliceActivity at 6.75M subscribers, Donut Media at 6.8M) were evaluated but deprioritized — PoliceActivity primarily shows unedited footage without legal analysis, and Donut Media is an automotive entertainment channel with limited rights-education content. Both were excluded from the final table on niche-fit grounds despite large reach.

---

## Top Niche Themes Observed

1. **Police accountability and bodycam analysis** is the largest and most engaged content category. Channels like Audit the Audit, The Civil Rights Lawyer, LackLuster, and Long Island Audit collectively represent millions of highly intent viewers who actively follow police rights issues.

2. **Legal education for everyday people** (Law By Mike, LegalEagle, Steve Lehto, Attorney Tom) reaches a broader audience with less acute intent but more mainstream scale. These channels are better for app awareness; the accountability channels are better for conversion.

3. **First Amendment audit content** (Long Island Audit, James Freeman) is a distinctive sub-niche where creators actively model the exact behavior this app supports — recording police interactions in real time. This is the highest-intent, most directly relevant niche for the app's recording and rights-education features.

4. **Dashcam and driver tech** (Vortex Radar, Dash Cam Owners Australia) is a tangential but genuine fit. These audiences already buy hardware to document drives; an app that adds legal intelligence is a logical upgrade sell.

## Notes on Methodology Limitations

- Subscriber counts and view stats were sourced from multiple analytics aggregators (vidIQ, Social Blade, SpeakRJ, HypeAuditor, youtubers.me, Tubics) as YouTube's rendered channel pages return JavaScript config data rather than readable HTML via automated fetch. Figures may vary by 5–15% across platforms depending on capture date.
- "Engagement rate" in this report uses views-based calculation (avg views / subscribers × 100), which is a higher figure than comment- or like-based engagement. Where platform-reported engagement scores were available (vidIQ, HypeAuditor), those are noted separately in the table.
- Average comments per video data was only reliably available for Law By Mike (2.1K/video) and LegalEagle (2K/video) from HypeAuditor. Other channels did not surface comment count data in public analytics results.
- Vortex Radar's subscriber count (162K confirmed from their own About page) places them below mega-tier; this is a targeted micro-to-mid channel but the audience quality justifies inclusion.
