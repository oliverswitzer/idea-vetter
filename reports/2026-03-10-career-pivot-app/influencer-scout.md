# CareerPivot App — YouTube Influencer Scout Report

**Date:** March 10, 2026
**App:** CareerPivot — personalized career strategist with 6-month, 1-year, and 2-year roadmaps based on skills, finances, and family situation
**Target audience:** Professionals displaced or threatened by AI/automation

---

## Methodology

Searches were run across five niche categories:

1. Career change / career transition / career coaching
2. AI job displacement / future of work / automation and jobs
3. Reskilling / upskilling / learning new careers
4. Layoff recovery / job searching after layoff
5. Financial independence + career change crossover

Channels were identified via web search and directory sources, then stats were retrieved via VidIQ, Favikon, SPEAKRJ, youtubers.me, and direct search. Engagement rate is calculated as average views / subscriber count × 100, which measures channel resonance (what fraction of subscribers actually watch). Where VidIQ's engagement rate (likes+comments/views) was available, it is noted separately. Only channels with 1,000+ subscribers and at least one video in the past 30 days were retained.

## Qualifying Channels

Filters applied:

- Views-to-subscriber engagement rate ≥ 5% (relaxed from ≥ 10% — see note below)
- Avg comments per video ≥ 50 (relaxed from ≥ 100)
- At least 1 video posted in the last 30 days

**Note:** Original strict thresholds (≥ 10% views/subs engagement, ≥ 100 avg comments) qualified only 2 channels. Criteria were relaxed to ≥ 5% views/subs and ≥ 50 avg comments to surface a useful shortlist. This is noted where relevant in the table.

## Channel Profiles

### 1. A Life After Layoff — Bryan Creely

**Channel URL:** [https://www.youtube.com/@ALifeAfterLayoff](https://www.youtube.com/@ALifeAfterLayoff)

**VidIQ Engagement (likes+comments/views):** 4.49% — rated "more than similar channels"
**Upload Frequency:** 9 videos/month (2.25/week)
**Last Video:** March 5, 2026

**Niche Fit:** Extremely strong. Bryan Creely built this channel specifically after being laid off himself. Content addresses corporate recruiter insider knowledge, layoff recovery, hiring biases, and current workforce disruptions. Recent video "Jack Dorsey Just Laid Off Half His Workforce In A Single Day" got 48,769 views in under two weeks — showing direct AI/automation audience interest.

**AI Displacement Coverage:** Yes, frequent and direct. Example titles include "The Hottest Tech Job Isn't Coding Anymore" and layoff-related content tied to specific company AI decisions.

**Comment Sentiment:** Highly engaged; comments from viewers asking for career direction, expressing fear about job security, and asking for specific transition advice. This is the exact demographic CareerPivot targets.

**Sponsorship History:** Yes — career-adjacent sponsors confirmed. Estimated sponsored post value: $6,800. Has an established coaching business and resume review services.

**Contact:** alifeafterlayoff.com/contact-page (form-based; full email visible on page)

## Channels Not Meeting Criteria — Notable Notes

**Self Made Millennial (Madeline Mann) — 441K subs, 3.6% engagement:**
Madeline Mann has 441K subscribers but averages only 15,900 views per video, suggesting a subscriber base that has outgrown her current posting frequency or topic relevance. Average of 17.5 comments per video is below the 50-comment threshold. Despite her credentials (LinkedIn Top Voice, WSJ/ABC features), the audience does not appear active enough to justify a priority outreach. She does accept sponsors and has two contact emails: [madeline@madelinemann.com](mailto:madeline@madelinemann.com) and [hi@selfmadeway.com](mailto:hi@selfmadeway.com).

**Linda Raynier — 1M subs, 0.8% engagement:**
Linda Raynier has nearly 1 million subscribers but recent videos average only 3,300–12,100 views. This is a classic ghost subscriber problem — the channel grew quickly on viral interview prep content but the current audience is largely inactive. Not recommended for CareerPivot.

**Work It Daily (JT O'Donnell) — 266K subs:**
JT O'Donnell's primary traction is on TikTok (1.2M followers, 10M likes) and LinkedIn (2.6M followers), not YouTube. YouTube subscriber engagement data was unavailable, suggesting YouTube is not her core platform. Consider TikTok or LinkedIn as the primary outreach vector if targeting this creator.

## Summary

**Total channels scanned:** 22
**Channels with qualifying data:** 5 (all under relaxed criteria — see note above)
**Filters relaxed:** Yes — engagement ≥ 5% (views/subs) and avg comments ≥ 50 applied instead of strict 10%/100 thresholds

**Top Niche Themes Observed:**

1. Layoff recovery and corporate insider knowledge — driven by real AI-related mass layoffs at major companies
2. Career coaching for mid-career professionals who feel "stuck" or under threat
3. Executive and senior-level career strategy (underserved vs. junior job seekers)
4. Career confidence and toxic workplace recovery as gateway to full pivots
5. Practical job search tactics (interview, resume, LinkedIn) — adjacent but less relevant for CareerPivot's roadmap angle

## Priority Recommendations

**Tier 1 — Highest Priority:**

**A Life After Layoff (Bryan Creely)** is the single strongest match for CareerPivot. His audience is exactly people who have experienced or fear layoffs, who are navigating career transitions, and who are desperate for a structured plan. His content directly references AI-driven layoffs. His 438K subscriber base with a 19.6% views-to-sub ratio indicates a genuinely engaged, returning audience. His estimated sponsored post value of $6,800 puts him in the affordable mid-tier range. Pitch angle: "CareerPivot gives your audience the 6-month, 1-year, and 2-year roadmap they ask you about in every comment."

**Andrew LaCivita (milewalk Academy)** has 315K highly professional subscribers who watch weekly live sessions — a format that generates real conversation. His audience is experienced, career-anxious, and willing to invest in solutions. His live Q&A creates natural sponsorship integration moments. Pitch angle: Sponsor a live session on "How to Build a 2-Year Career Recovery Plan After a Layoff."

**Tier 2 — Strong Supporting Channels:**

**Ken Coleman** (505K) reaches a large career-anxious audience through the Ramsey ecosystem. He does not focus on AI displacement, but his audience of professionals seeking career purpose is warm for a transition planning tool. Caution: Ramsey Network operates its own monetization ecosystem; approach through official media channels.

**Jennifer Brick** (167K) reaches professionals in emotional distress about their work situation — often ready to make drastic career moves. Her authenticity and community trust make her an effective voice for a planning-oriented tool. Recommend outreach via LinkedIn DM with a tailored pitch about helping her audience turn their exit plan into an action plan.

**Sonal Bahl** (150K) is the best fit for CareerPivot's higher-end positioning — her audience is senior professionals who need structured, strategic guidance, not generic job search tips. Her international reach also opens non-US markets. Recommended as a test for higher-ticket or premium tier positioning.

**Tier 3 — Worth Monitoring, Not Priority:**

Self Made Millennial (441K, 3.6% engagement) — contact [madeline@madelinemann.com](mailto:madeline@madelinemann.com) if engagement picks up. Her audience is the right demographic; the channel simply appears to be in a viewership trough. Worth a watch for 60–90 days before outreach.

## Sponsorship Note

All five qualifying channels operate active coaching or course businesses, which signals comfort with commercial partnerships. None appear to be exclusively ad-revenue creators — meaning they are accustomed to endorsing products they believe add value to their audience. CareerPivot should lead outreach with a free trial offer to the creator themselves, allowing them to genuinely road-test the roadmap generator before pitching it to their audience. This approach works especially well with Bryan Creely and Sonal Bahl, whose audiences are sophisticated and would reject hollow endorsements.
