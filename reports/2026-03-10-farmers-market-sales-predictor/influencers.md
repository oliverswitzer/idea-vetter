# MarketPrep — Influencer Research Report
**App:** MarketPrep (Square POS-integrated sales predictor for farmers market vendors)
**Target audience:** Farmers market vendors — bakers, produce farmers, craft food sellers, artisans
**Research date:** 2026-03-10

---

## Research Summary

- **Total channels scanned:** ~28 channels identified and evaluated
- **Channels with enough data to score:** 12
- **Qualifying channels (primary filters — engagement ≥ 10%, avg comments ≥ 100, active in last 30 days):** 2
- **Qualifying channels (relaxed filters — engagement ≥ 5%, avg comments ≥ 50):** 7 additional channels
- **Note:** Relaxed filters applied — this niche skews toward smaller, tighter-knit creator communities. The primary 10% / 100-comment threshold is a high bar for agriculture and food business YouTubers where engagement is strong but absolute comment volumes are lower than entertainment channels. Relaxed criteria noted where applied.

---

## Methodology Notes

YouTube channel pages return only JavaScript configuration data and cannot be scraped for rendered stats. All engagement data was sourced from vidIQ, youtubers.me, SPEAKRJ, Feedspot, and stated figures from creator websites and press materials. Engagement rate is calculated as (avg views / subscribers) x 100 where raw view data was available. Comment counts are approximated from per-video engagement ratios where disclosed. Some channels had insufficient public data and are noted as "data limited."

## Top Niche Themes Observed

1. **Homestead-to-market pipeline:** Channels like Roots and Refuge, Our Wyoming Life, and Acre Homestead all document growing food with the implied or explicit intent of selling surplus. This is where the MarketPrep pitch (help me know what to pack) lands naturally.

2. **Cottage food operator entrepreneurship:** South Street Cookies and Marketing Food Online represent a distinct sub-niche of home-based food businesses scaling through market appearances. These audiences are the most transaction-minded and tool-receptive.

3. **Market gardening profitability:** Urban Farmer Curtis Stone, Neversink Farm, and Nature's Always Right represent the produce-farm side, focused on yield-per-acre economics that translate directly to inventory planning concerns.

4. **Square POS content is not yet a distinct creator category** for farmers market vendors. No channel was found focused specifically on Square POS for market vendors. This is an opportunity — MarketPrep content around Square integration could generate organic search traffic in an underserved space.

## Recommended Priority Creators

### Priority 1 — Highest ROI Outreach Targets

**South Street Cookies (Tanya Clowers)**
The clearest match for MarketPrep in the cottage food / farmers market baker segment. She is an actual market vendor, creates business-tips content alongside tutorials, and has a proven YouTube audience. Her content is about running the business, not just baking — which is exactly where MarketPrep belongs. Estimated engagement is the highest of any channel found. Contact via website form.

**Our Wyoming Life (Erin Galloway)**
Erin actively sells produce at farmers markets in Wyoming, makes it a central part of her channel identity, and has a highly engaged audience (8.86% engagement). Lower upload frequency but each video gets strong traction. Strong community around the vendor experience.

**Roots and Refuge Farm (Jess Sowards)**
Largest qualifying channel. Engagement is at 10.7% — among the highest of any agricultural channel this size. Audience has deep interest in food growing for market. Previous farmers market vendor. Community is active and comments-heavy. Best choice for broad-reach campaign.

### Priority 2 — Strong Secondary Targets

**Marketing Food Online (Damian Roberti)**
Confirmed business email (marketingfoodonline@yahoo.com), entrepreneurial audience, high upload frequency, and explicitly covers farmers market selling as a topic. Lowest engagement of the qualifying group but the most direct commercial intent. Good for a sponsored segment or product mention in a "Square POS tools for food businesses" video.

**Acre Homestead (Becky Jones)**
Massive reach (911K subs, 256K avg views). Audience is more consumer-skewed but the volume of potential vendor viewers is large in absolute terms. Best for top-of-funnel brand awareness. No confirmed contact email — requires cold outreach via channel or website.
